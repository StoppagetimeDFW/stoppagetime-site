Episode 10

Fall check-in, futsal & team managers

Fall season check-in, the case for futsal, and what being a team manager really takes.

About this episode

We're past the midpoint of the fall season, so Colby and Cable check in on how it's going across DFW youth soccer: what's working, what's not, and what families should watch for down the stretch.

Then we get into futsal. What is it, why do so many top players swear by it, and is it worth adding to your kid's winter? Finally, we cover one of the most underappreciated jobs in club soccer, the team manager. We break down what the role actually involves, from schedules and communication to paperwork and keeping parents on the same page, and what to know before you raise your hand to volunteer.
